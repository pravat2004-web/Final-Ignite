# IGNITE visual direction

## Theme Name
Cosmic Neon Gratitude

## Intro
A pre-event invitation experience that feels like a midnight stage before the lights come up: electric cyan and violet energy over a deep black canvas, softened by warm gold and pink celebration cues.

## Design movement
The page moves from a focused cosmic loading ritual into a confident, spacious event story. Sections feel like transparent display panels floating over an active night-sky field.

## Core principles
Clarity before spectacle; one strong visual anchor per section; short responsive paths for touch; motion that explains state; glow used to signal importance rather than decorate every surface.

## Color philosophy
Black and navy create depth. Cyan is the primary interactive signal, violet/purple carries the IGNITE atmosphere, gold highlights the upcoming celebration, and pink/orange marks warmth, anticipation, and the university identity.

## Layout paradigm
A centered single-column narrative with generous vertical rhythm, max-width content shells, auto-fit cards, and a hero that feels like a poster while remaining readable at 320px.

## Signature elements
Cosmic canvas particles, a thin gradient progress ring, glass cards with low-opacity borders, Orbitron wordmarks, animated gradient section headings, rounded pill controls, and the mouse-shaped scroll hint.

## Interaction philosophy
Interactions should acknowledge the user's action immediately, with motion feedback and readable status messages. The countdown, registration notice, and unavailable states stay explicit; no control pretends that the event has already happened.

## Animation
Use short staged entrances, gentle floating, restrained hover lift, pulsing separators/hearts, and smooth modal transitions. All animation frames, intervals, and listeners are cleaned up.

## Typography system
Orbitron for display and countdown, Rajdhani for labels and section subtitles, Montserrat for button/form language, and Inter for longer body copy. Sizes use clamp() so the composition remains stable on mobile.

## Brand essence
IGNITE is a fresh start: grateful, energetic, inclusive, and lightly futuristic.

## Brand voice
Warm, concise, celebratory, and direct. Copy builds anticipation for the 5 October poster release and 9 October main event.

## Wordmark/logo
Use the compact project mark at `/ignite-mark.svg`: a solid cyan-violet geometric N/orbit silhouette on an opaque navy square for favicon and managed project branding. An optional `/ignite-logo.png` can be added later as a header asset.

## Signature brand color
Neon cyan `#4de7ff`.
